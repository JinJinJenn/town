Your visit to Town

Settle in.

Drag to explore, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag to rotate. Use WASD or the arrow keys to move, Q and E to turn, and R to reset the camera.

Visit Clover Cottage to open the roof and invite Luca onto the sofa. Browse Residents to find someone, or open photo mode to save a picture of the world without the website panels.

Lighter rendering reduces pixel count. The shading switch below is separate.

The same seed recreates the same neighborhood. Town currently has 16 lots and one furnished, interactive home; it is not yet a full life simulator.

About Jenn

Hi, I'm Jenn.

I make Sims 4 mods for my own game and share the projects I'm working on. Town is one of those experiments: a place to wander, meet a few residents, and watch a little neighborhood come to life.

The workshop on my mods site has details about each project and its testing status. This town is still growing, so the things you can do here will expand as the game develops.
