About this neighborhood

Make yourself at home.

Drag the scene to move around, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag to rotate. On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys to move, Q and E to turn, and R to return to the starting view.

Select a resident to follow their walk, or select a building to learn which kind of home the generator created. Tour moves the camera for you, while Pause stops the residents.

This checkpoint contains a finite neighborhood with procedural buildings and walking residents. Furnished interiors, decorating, and an expanding world are planned, not implemented here yet.

The same seed produces the same neighborhood. Changes here do not affect Jenn's other games or her website.
